1 Overview & Core Philosophy

Dhvani ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is an offline-first, standalone media player engineered with uncompromising respect for user sovereignty and privacy. Our business model does not rely on advertising, telemetry collection, or selling consumer profiles.

We collect, store, transmit, and sell zero personally identifiable information. The music on your device belongs entirely to you.

2 What Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or biometric data.
  • No Device Tracking: We do not capture Advertising IDs (GAID), IMEI, hardware identifiers, or IP logs.
  • No Analytics SDKs: Dhvani contains no third-party tracking scripts, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDKs, or behavioural profiling suites.
  • No Advertisements: The application is completely ad-free.

3 Device Permissions and Purpose

To deliver an audio listening experience, Dhvani requests specific device capabilities strictly confined to their stated utility:

  • Audio & Media Storage (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Required to scan, index, and playback your locally stored music files, parse ID3 tags, and extract cover art. Dhvani only reads audio formats and never inspects personal photos, videos, or unrelated documents.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK): Mandated by Android operating systems to ensure uninterrupted audio streaming when Dhvani runs in the background or when your screen is locked.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Utilized on Android 13+ solely to render lockscreen and notification drawer playback media controls (Play, Pause, Skip, Track Title, and Artwork).
  • Wake Lock (WAKE_LOCK): Prevents your device processor from sleeping while audio is actively decoding.

4 Network Communication (Lyrics Retrieval)

Dhvani is fundamentally an offline application. The sole optional feature that accesses the Internet is our synchronized lyrics fetcher:

When you view lyrics or tap "Auto-fetch from LRCLIB", Dhvani transmits an anonymous HTTPS request to the public LRCLIB API containing only the track's title, artist name, and duration. This query carries no account credentials, device identifiers, or tracking tokens.

5 Data Storage & Security

All playlists, favorites, play counters, listening history, custom equalizer configurations, and ID3 tag edits are stored exclusively inside local databases (such as ObjectBox and SharedPreferences) located on your physical hardware. Removing the application permanently deletes this local cache.

6 Children's Privacy

Dhvani does not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Because no data is harvested from any user, Dhvani is safe for audiences of all ages and complies with COPPA and GDPR regulations.
