Privacy Policy

Updated • July 23, 2026

Vikash Choudhary ("we," "us," or "our") operates the To-Do & Grocery mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information. Our core principle is that your data belongs to you. We do not run external databases to collect your tasks, and we never sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • User Content: The tasks, grocery items, and preferences you create. This data is stored strictly locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on our servers.

B. Device Permissions & Local Processing

Our app requests the following device permissions to provide specific features. Data processed by these sensors is handled locally and is never transmitted over the internet by our Service:

  • Camera: Used exclusively for scanning barcodes or QR codes locally on your device.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition: Used for speech-to-text input features. Audio data is processed by your device's native speech recognition APIs.
  • Biometrics (FaceID/TouchID): Used for locking the app and protecting your data. Biometric authentication is handled securely by your device's OS; we do not have access to your biometric templates.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the local information on your device strictly to operate the Service:

  • To provide and maintain the app's core to-do and grocery tracking functionality.
  • To allow you to manually export and import encrypted backups of your own data.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data (tasks, lists, settings) is stored locally on your device using industry-standard encrypted database technology (SQLCipher). You have the option to manually export an encrypted backup file to your local file system. We have absolutely no access to your data or your backups.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored exclusively on your device, you have complete control over it. You can permanently delete all your data at any time by simply uninstalling the app or clearing the app's data in your device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

The Service utilizes standard operating system APIs (such as Google Play Services on Android) for core functionality like speech recognition. These native services operate under their respective platform privacy policies. We do not use third-party analytics (like Firebase Analytics) or advertising networks.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
